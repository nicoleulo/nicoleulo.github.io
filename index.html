About the App

How to Use

Getting started takes less than a minute:

1. Enter Your Salary or Hourly Rate

Input your annual salary or hourly wage. You can also set your pay frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly).

2. Select Your Filing Status & State

Choose your tax filing status (single, married, etc.) and the state you work in so the app can apply the correct tax rates.

3. Add Deductions (Optional)

Include pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions or health insurance premiums for a more precise estimate.

4. View Your Take Home Pay

The app instantly shows your estimated net pay per paycheck and annually, along with a full tax breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app free?

Yes, the core calculator is free to use.

Are the tax calculations exact?

The app provides estimates based on standard federal and state tax brackets. Individual situations vary — always consult a tax professional for precise figures.

Does the app store my personal information?

Payroll inputs and calculations stay on your device in the calculator flow. Production advertising, Apple TestFlight reporting, and information you choose to send in feedback or support messages are separate. See our Privacy Policy for details, and redact payroll information before sharing screenshots.

Which states are supported?

The app supports all 50 U.S. states, including states with no income tax.

My calculation seems off — what should I do?

Double-check your filing status, state, and pay frequency. If something still looks wrong, please contact us and we'll look into it.

About this policy

Take Home Pay Calculator is provided by nicole ulogar. This policy covers the production app and the separate Take Home Pay Calculator Beta distributed through Apple TestFlight. Their advertising behavior differs, as explained below.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by the developer, contact nicoleu1999@gmail.com.

Payroll calculations on your device

Payroll calculations and entered amounts remain on your device in the calculator's input and calculation flow. This flow does not send your salary, withholding entries, deductions or calculated results to our servers. You do not need to provide your name to perform a calculation.

This local processing is separate from information collected through advertising in the production app, Apple's TestFlight service, or information you voluntarily send us. If you include payroll details in a support email, feedback comment or screenshot, those details leave your device as part of that communication.

Please use sample figures when reporting issues and redact personal payroll information from screenshots before sharing them.

Advertising in production and the ad-free beta

The production app may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising services may process advertising-related usage information and device identifiers, subject to applicable consent requirements and your App Tracking Transparency choices. ATT choices do not replace other required advertising consent.

The separate TestFlight beta does not initialize AdMob, request advertisements or perform AdMob measurement. It does not request ATT permission for advertising. No additional analytics services are used in this beta.

The absence of advertising in the beta does not disable Apple's TestFlight reporting.

Analytics and identifiable information

Aggregate statistics are different from the underlying information used to produce them. Advertising services in production and Apple's TestFlight reporting may involve information associated with a device or an identifiable person. We therefore do not claim that all analytics or diagnostic information is anonymous or incapable of identifying you.

Apple's TestFlight reporting

Apple automatically collects crash and usage information from TestFlight beta testing and shares it with the developer. TestFlight does not offer an opt-out from this collection. Reports may include app/device details and tester names or email addresses; public-link-only invitations limit name and email visibility to the developer.

Optional TestFlight feedback, including comments and screenshots, is received by Apple and the developer and may identify you. Screenshots can reveal payroll information. Review and redact what you submit.

Apple's TestFlight privacy notice explains its processing and controls. Apple states that it keeps feedback for one year and may retain crash and usage information until bugs are resolved. These are Apple's retention practices, not the developer's retention schedule. We do not control or promise deletion of Apple's records.

How we use and retain developer-held beta information

Apple makes TestFlight reports and feedback available to the developer through its developer services. We also receive information that you choose to send directly to our support email. These channels are separate from the calculator's local payroll processing.

We use tester feedback only to investigate bugs, improve the calculator and respond to testers. Access to developer-held feedback is limited to Nicole and Libby, with additional maintainers permitted only when explicitly authorized. Apple's restrictions on third-party sharing of TestFlight reports and feedback continue to apply; this access statement does not authorize sharing that Apple prohibits.

We use the designated support email and Apple's TestFlight and App Store Connect systems to handle support and testing information. We avoid unnecessary local downloads.

We keep developer-held information only as long as reasonably needed for support, testing and resolving issues. This includes support emails, downloaded diagnostics, feedback and screenshots. When that information is no longer needed, we delete the information and copies under our control, including accessible backups where feasible. This is a purpose-based retention rule, not a fixed period, and does not determine how long Apple keeps information in its services.
